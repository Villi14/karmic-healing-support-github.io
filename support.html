Common Solutions

  • Notifications are not appearing: Open Settings → Apps → Karmic Healing → Notifications and allow notifications. Also check that the reminder is enabled and scheduled for a future time.
  • Reminders arrive late on Android: Android may deliver reminders a few minutes after the set time to save battery. If they are delayed much longer, open Settings → Apps → Karmic Healing → Battery and allow unrestricted battery use.
  • Sound or vibration is not working: Check the in-app session settings, your device volume, and Silent Mode, Focus, or Do Not Disturb settings.
  • Data is not saving: Make sure your device has available storage, then close and reopen the app. Your requests, reminders, and notes are stored locally on the device.
  • The app language is incorrect: In the app, open Settings → Change Language. On iPhone and iPad this opens Settings → Apps → Karmic Healing → Language; on Android you choose the language directly in the app.
  • Locked out of the app: If Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint does not work, unlock the app with the passcode you chose when turning on the app lock.
